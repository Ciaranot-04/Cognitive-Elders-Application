About Us

Shapes is built to support both elderly individuals and those who care for them. With simple daily activities and progress tracking, it helps maintain cognitive health while giving carers clear insight into development over time.

Get started with Shapes

Getting started with Shapes is carefully designed to be as simple as possible so it's more accessible to elderly people who are not as well-acquainted with technology. From the moment a user opens the app they are guided into short daily activities that test key cognitive areas such as memory, linguistics, attention and problem-solving. As users continue to interact with the app, Shapes adapts accordingly to their individual performance, allowing users to track their progress and notice areas that need more attention.



For carers, Shapes provides an easy way to stay connected and informed. Through a dedicated care account they can access the user's statistics and gain insight into how the user is developing over time, helping them recognise improvements or identify potential concerns early.
